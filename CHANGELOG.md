## [1.0.0](https://github.com/comprofix/gemburrow/commits/v1.0.0) - 2026-10-31

The first release of Gem Burrow.

### Added

- Dig through 8 original cavern layouts as a dwarf, which then repeat with a rising level ramp
- Finish a level by clearing every jewel, or by destroying all of its kobolds
- Collect jewels for points, with a 250-point bonus for eight in quick succession
- Push and drop money bags to crush kobolds, and grab the gold when they burst
- Fire a shot that has to recharge, and takes longer to recharge on later levels
- Outwit kobolds that turn impatient and swell into goblins, which dig through earth and eat jewels
- Take the glowing mushroom when it appears, then chase and eat the fleeing kobolds for doubling points
- Earn extra lives every 20,000 points, or from a rare tankard of ale from level 3
- Choose between Easy, Normal and Hard, with a high score table
- Switch between two looks in Options: Smooth and Modern pixel art
- Listen to an original theme and sound effects, with a funeral march and a headstone when the dwarf dies
- Play on Windows and Linux (64-bit), with the keyboard in play and keys or mouse in the menus
